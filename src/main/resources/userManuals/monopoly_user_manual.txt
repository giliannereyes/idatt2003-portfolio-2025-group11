Monopoly: Game Manual

Objective:
Become the wealthiest player by buying properties, collecting rent, and avoiding bankruptcy.

Game Rules:
1. 2-5 players can participate.
2. Each player starts with $300.
3. Players take turns rolling two dice and moving their token clockwise around the board.
4. If you land on an unowned property, you can buy it for the price listed on the space.
5. If you land on a property owned by another player, you must pay them rent.
6. When you land on or pass "Go", you collect $50.
7. If you land on "Go to Jail", you will go directly to jail and miss your next turn.
8. If you land on the "Jail" space, you will miss your next turn.
9. If you land on "Free Parking", nothing happens.

Winning condition:
1. The game ends when all but one player go bankrupt.

Limitations:
1. There are no chance or community chest cards in this version.
2. There are no upgrades or houses.
3. Properties cannot be traded or sold.

Instructions:
Players can throw the dice by clicking on the button "Throw Dice".
The game will automatically update the position of the players on the board.
If a property can be purchased, a window will pop up asking if you want to buy it.
