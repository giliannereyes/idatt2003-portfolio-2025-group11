Ladders Game: Game Manual

Objective:
Reach the finish line first by climbing ladders and avoiding snakes.

Game Rules:
1. 2-5 players can participate.
2. Each player starts with a token on the start space.
3. Players take turns rolling a die and moving their token forward the number of spaces shown on the die.
4. If you land on a ladder, you can climb up to the space at the top of the ladder.
5. If you land on a snake, you must slide down to the space at the bottom of the snake.

Special Rules:
If you land on a space with the following color:
- Violet: You will skip your next turn.
- Yellow: You will move back to the start space.

Winning condition:
1. The game ends when the first player reaches the finish line.

Instructions:
Players can throw the dice by clicking on the button "Throw Dice".
The game will automatically update the position of the players on the board.
